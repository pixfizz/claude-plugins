Load the `studio-designs` skill from this plugin and run its workflow.

Start by asking which product or studio the designs are for and whether the files are layered (PSD) or finished flat files (PDF, PNG, JPG), then get the files and one design export from the target template. Pick the route from the skill's table and say plainly when a studio cannot take a design yet. For own calendars or own photo books, or designs already on the site, no designer file is needed: start from the designs and templates on the site.

If the ask is to change how a design looks before the customer picks it (a thumbnail, a preview image, the calendar spiral, a border instead of full bleed), read `references/previews.md` first and say where that picture comes from before changing anything.

Show the proofs and the previews before building or importing anything. Never stretch artwork to fit a template, never type an export by hand, and never change studio settings, design codes or product codes to fit a design. Do not set prices. Report every check as verified or not verified.
