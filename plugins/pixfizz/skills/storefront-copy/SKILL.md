## Hard Rules

These rules apply to ALL copy output. No exceptions.

### Hero Section
- Headline: 5-10 words maximum. Maximum 2 lines on mobile.
- Lead with outcome/benefit, never features.
- Use the Eyebrow Technique when appropriate: short category line above main headline.
- Subheadline: 1-2 lines. Formula: "For [target audience] who want to [solve pain / achieve result]."
- One primary CTA (visually dominant) plus optionally one secondary CTA (visually subordinate).
- Always include micro-proof adjacent to or directly below the CTA.
- Hero visual: show the product doing its job (UI screenshot, workflow, storefront). Never stock photos, abstract shapes, or dashboard dumps.
- Mobile: CTA buttons min 44-48px height, full-width. Text and CTA above hero visual in stacked layout.

### CTA Copy
- Always use specific action verbs stating what user GETS, not what they DO.
- DO: "Start your free trial", "Book a 15-minute demo", "See client storefronts"
- NEVER: "Submit", "Click here", "Learn more"
- Repeat identical CTA wording every time it appears on the page.
- One primary conversion goal per page. Secondary CTAs must be visually subordinate.
- Place CTAs: hero, mid-page (after 1-2 feature sections), and conversion close at bottom.

### Social Proof
- Micro-proof (star rating, logos, short metric) must appear adjacent to every CTA placement.
- Testimonials must include: real name, title, company. Before-and-after structure preferred.
- Quantitative metrics must use specific, verifiable numbers. Never "thousands of customers."
- Never use self-praise ("We're the best"). Social proof requires external/collective validation.
- Never hide reviews in images (invisible to search engines).

### Copy Tone
- Lead with pain, not potential. People act faster to avoid loss than to gain benefit.
- Specificity beats abstraction. Concrete details, verifiable facts, visual language.
- Benefits over features. Every feature statement must be translated to an outcome.
- Short paragraphs (max 2 lines). Mix sentence lengths for rhythm.
- No buzzwords: "powerful", "innovative", "seamless", "cutting-edge", "next-generation."

## Page Structure Frameworks

### For a storefront homepage (Recommended: 4C Blueprint)

1. **Clarity** (Hero): Eyebrow + outcome headline + audience subheadline + primary CTA + micro-proof
2. **Comprehension** (How It Works): Features mapped to the customer's workflow (e.g. Choose > Personalise > Order > Collect or Receive)
3. **Credibility** (Social Proof): Testimonials, metrics, case study snippets, FAQ section
4. **Conversion** (Close): Final CTA + off-ramp for visitors not ready to convert

### For Feature/Product Landing Pages (Recommended: PAS)

1. **Problem**: Identify specific customer pain point
2. **Agitation**: Amplify emotional impact of the problem
3. **Solution**: Present the product/feature as the remedy with CTA

### For Long-form Marketing Pages (Recommended: PRESTO)

1. **Pain**: Hook by addressing operational pain
2. **Resonate**: Build emotional rapport through storytelling
3. **Educate**: Features mapped to workflows
4. **Simplify**: Bold visual section resetting analytical visitors to action-driven state
5. **Testify**: Social proof section
6. **Offer**: CTA and/or lead magnet

## Hook Types Quick Reference

When writing headlines, subheadlines, or section openers, select from these patterns:

### For Hero Headlines
- **Eyebrow (Category + Benefit)**: Category line + outcome headline
- **Threat / Pain Call-Out**: Urgent warning or frustrating problem
- **Direct Command**: Forceful imperative ("Stop X and start Y")
- **Contradiction / Tension**: Conflicting idea forcing reader to pause
- **Breakthrough / Novelty**: "Announcing" / "Introducing" language

### For Subheadlines
- **Identity Trigger**: "For [audience] who want to [outcome]"
- **ROMO (Relief of Missing Out)**: "You don't need [bloated thing]. Just [simple thing]."

### For Section Openers
- **Falsifiable Fact**: Specific stat comparing industry average to product outcome
- **FOMO / Social Proof**: "Join [number]+ [audience] who already [benefit]"
- **Confirm Beliefs Pivot**: "You already know [frustration]. Here's how to fix it."
- **Curiosity Gap**: "How a [specific underdog] uses [feature] to [massive outcome]"

### For CTA Areas
- **Objection-Killer**: "[Action CTA]. No [friction point]."
- **Micro-Proof Booster**: "Trusted by [number]+ [audience]"

## Scroll & Retention Patterns

- **Slippery Slide**: Every line's sole purpose is to make the reader want to read the next line.
- **Causal connectors**: Use "as a result of", "because of" between sections, not "then" or "next."
- **Workflow mapping**: Organize feature sections to mirror the customer's actual daily task sequence.
- **"Simplify" reset**: After dense educational content, insert a bold visual section to reset from analytical to emotional state.
- **Off-ramp**: Near conversion close, provide low-commitment alternative (guide download, case study, pricing page) for visitors not ready to buy.
- **Section 2 as scroll-stopper**: Treat the section after the hero as a brand new hook, not just a continuation.
- **Cliffhangers**: Use setups that lack immediate payoff at section endings to force scrolling.

## Common Mistakes Checklist

Before finalizing any page copy, verify none of these are present:

- [ ] Headline over 10 words or over 2 lines on mobile
- [ ] Leading with features instead of outcomes/benefits
- [ ] Vague/abstract language ("powerful", "innovative", "seamless")
- [ ] CTA says "Submit", "Click here", or "Learn more"
- [ ] Multiple different CTA labels on the same page
- [ ] No social proof adjacent to CTA buttons
- [ ] Anonymous testimonials (missing name, title, company)
- [ ] Self-praise without external validation
- [ ] Stock photos or abstract art instead of product UI
- [ ] No off-ramp for visitors not ready to convert
- [ ] CTA pushed below mobile fold by long headline
- [ ] Images stacking above text on mobile
- [ ] Navigation links and social icons cluttering conversion page
- [ ] Messaging mismatch between traffic source and page content
- [ ] AI-generated copy used as-is without human editing
